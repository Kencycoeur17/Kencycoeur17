## What I build

I am a Haiti-based digital entrepreneur, full-stack developer and technology consultant. I design and improve products in **fintech, web applications, AI/LLMs, automation, cybersecurity and digital services**.

My engineering priorities are **security, reliable integrations, clear mobile experiences and maintainable architecture**. I favor a focused MVP before adding complexity.

## How I approach projects

1. **Validate the problem:** clarify the user journey, business value and MVP scope.
2. **Design for trust:** protect credentials, customer information and sensitive workflows.
3. **Build for mobile:** prioritize usable interfaces and realistic connectivity conditions.
4. **Operate reliably:** document deployment, monitor failures and test critical flows.
5. **Scale deliberately:** add complexity when the product and evidence justify it.
